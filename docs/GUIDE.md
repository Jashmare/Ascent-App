# How to use Ascent

Ascent organises your plans by altitude, from what you'll do today up to the dreams you're
reaching for. Each level can point to the one above it, so you can always see what something is
for. The same guide is inside the app (the **?** button at the top of Camp, Ridge and Summit),
and Settings → Guide → **Take the tour** walks you through it on screen.

| Altitude | What lives there | Time horizon |
|---|---|---|
| Camp | Daily tasks and routines | Today |
| Ridge | Objectives | This week, month or quarter |
| Summit | Long-term goals | A year or more |
| Sky | Dreams, and everything you've reached | No deadline |

Move between them with the bar at the bottom of the screen (on a wide screen, the altitude rail
on the left).

## 1. First run

1. **Start with the sky.** Write one thing you dream of and choose **Add to my sky**, or **Skip
   for now**.
2. **And one thing to do today.** Write it and choose **Add task**, or skip.
3. You land on Camp, with your dream in the strip of night sky at the top. Choose **Take the
   tour** for a one-minute walkthrough.

Moving from another phone or browser? Choose **Restore a backup** on the welcome screen instead.

## 2. Camp: today

- **Add a task:** type in "Add a task for today" and press Enter.
- **Make it a routine, or link it:** before pressing Enter, open the sliders button. Pick
  **Daily**, **Weekdays** or **Chosen days**, and optionally an objective it moves forward.
- **Check it off:** tap the task. It adds **10 m** to your altimeter; tap again to undo.
- **Reorder:** drag the grip (⠿) up or down. With a keyboard, focus the grip and press the up
  and down arrows. The ⋯ button edits, moves or deletes a task.
- **Unfinished yesterday?** Camp asks whether to **Bring them to today** or **Let them go**.
  Letting go archives them; nothing is deleted.
- **The climb line** rises as you finish today's tasks ("3 of 5 done"). Your **streak** counts the
  days on which everything planned got done. Days with nothing planned don't count either way, and
  when a streak ends Camp simply says "Fresh start today."
- **Above and below:** the night-sky strip shows one dream each day (tap it to visit). **Next on
  the ridge** shows the objective due soonest, and anything due today is listed at the top.

## 3. Ridge: objectives

- **Add an objective:** type it and press Enter. It's set for this month. The sliders button sets a
  horizon (this week, month, quarter or someday), a due date and a linked summit.
- Objectives are grouped as **Overdue, This week, This month, This quarter and Someday**, with due
  dates in plain words such as "3 days left" or "2 days overdue".
- **Tap an objective** to edit it, see its linked tasks, or add a task for today that links to it.
- **Check it off** to add **100 m**. Finished objectives move to **Done**, where you can reopen
  them.

## 4. Summit: long-term goals

1. **Add a summit:** type it and press Enter. Its sheet opens.
2. Add **why it matters**, a **target month**, the **dream it leads toward**, and **milestones**.
3. **Progress is automatic:** finished milestones and finished linked objectives, out of all of
   them. Switch to **Manual** for goals that don't break down neatly, and set it with the slider.
   The peak next to each summit fills like a snowline as you climb.
4. Each milestone you check off adds **250 m**.
5. **Reach the summit:** at 100% a button appears (it's always in the ⋯ menu too). Write how it
   feels to be there if you like, then watch it rise into your sky as a gold star: **1,000 m**.

## 5. Sky: dreams

- **Add a dream** with the button at the bottom of the Sky: a title, why it matters, what it looks
  like when it's real ("Picture it."), and a photo if you like. No deadlines, no percentages.
- **Pale stars** are dreams you're still reaching for. **Gold stars** are summits and dreams you've
  reached, joined by lines in the order you reached them: your constellation.
- **The horizon** has a peak for each active summit, as tall as its progress. Tap a peak to open
  it.
- **Tap a star** to open it: link a summit as a path toward it, **Mark as reached**, or move it back
  to dreaming.
- **List** shows the whole sky as lists: still reaching, reached, and on the horizon.

## 6. The altimeter

| You… | Altitude |
|---|---|
| Finish a task | +10 m |
| Finish an objective | +100 m |
| Finish a milestone | +250 m |
| Reach a summit | +1,000 m |

Camp, Ridge and Summit show today's climb and your lifetime total. Uncheck something and its
metres come off. Switch to feet in Settings.

## 7. Reminders and the weekly review

Set them in **Settings → Reminders**:

| Reminder | Default | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | On, 7:00 | "Today's climb: 4 tasks. Still reaching for: a studio by the sea." |
| Evening check-in | Off, 20:00 | Only when tasks are left: "2 tasks left today. Still time." |
| Objectives due | On | The day before and the morning of |
| Weekly review | On, Sunday 18:00 | "Time to look at the week from the ridge." |
| Dream nudge | Off, weekly | One dream's "picture it" note |

- **Turn on notifications** to get them while Ascent is open or in the background. Otherwise they
  appear inside the app.
- **Add to calendar** saves your routines, due dates and weekly review as a calendar file. Open it on
  your phone and your calendar reminds you even when Ascent is closed.
- **The weekly review** is a short guided look at the week. On review day Camp offers **Start the
  review**:
  1. what got done on the ridge,
  2. summit progress,
  3. one dream to revisit,
  4. next week's objectives.

## 8. Your data

- Everything stays on this device. There are no accounts and nothing is uploaded.
- **Back up data** (Settings) saves one file with everything, photos included.
- **Restore a backup** shows what's in the file, then **Replace** what's here or **Merge** with it.
- **Reset everything** deletes all data on this device, after you type "reset".
- **Install** from your browser's menu ("Install app", or "Add to Home Screen" on iPhone). After
  the first visit Ascent works offline.

## 9. Keyboard

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| Tab | Move between controls |
| Enter | Add what you typed, or press a button |
| Space | Check or uncheck |
| Up and down arrows | Reorder a task while its grip has focus |
| Escape | Close a sheet, end the tour, or skip the reaching ceremony |
